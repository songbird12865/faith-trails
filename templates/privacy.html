Faith-Trails

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Faith-Trails: A Closer Walk for Kids is an interactive Bible adventure designed for children. Protecting the privacy of children and families who use Faith-Trails is important to us.

Information Used by Faith-Trails

Faith-Trails does not require a child to create an account or provide an email address, phone number, mailing address, date of birth, or other contact information.

Players choose a player name or nickname so the game can save their progress. The name does not have to be the player's real name, and Faith-Trails does not verify the identity of a player.

Faith-Trails stores the player's chosen name, selected difficulty level, and game progress, such as quests and badges completed. This information is used only to provide game functionality and allow players to continue their Faith-Trails progress.

For parents and guardians: We encourage children to use a nickname or other non-identifying player name instead of their full legal name.

How Information Is Used

Player information is used only to operate Faith-Trails and save game progress. Faith-Trails does not sell player information, use it for advertising, or use it to create advertising profiles.

Sharing of Information

Faith-Trails does not provide player names or game progress to advertisers or sell this information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

Faith-Trails is designed for children and does not require children to provide personal contact information to play. Parents and guardians are encouraged to have children use a nickname or other non-identifying player name rather than a child's full legal name.

Deleting a Player

A saved player can be deleted using the player management controls within Faith-Trails. Deleting a player removes that player's saved profile and associated badge progress from the Faith-Trails game database.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information used by Faith-Trails. However, no Internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Faith-Trails features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or Faith-Trails privacy practices may be sent to Mel@acloserwalk.org.
